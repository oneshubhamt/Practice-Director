Memory schedule

You never need this page. It shows how the app decides when something comes back, and holds the one thing here you can undo: resting an item.

The one rule

Each item comes back at about the moment you would still recall it 9 times in 10.
Recall it whole and the next gap is longer. Miss part of it and the gap is shorter, with the missed points first. That is all the schedule does.
The 9 in 10 is fixed. The alternative that was tested cost about 18% more sessions for the same result.
